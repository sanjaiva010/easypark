What EasyPark does

Built like the real thing

Every screen in this prototype is backed by the same mock data layer, so it is ready to be connected to a real database and the ESP32 sensor network.

Real-time slot awareness

Colour-coded spots refresh instantly: green available, red occupied, amber reserved, grey under maintenance.

QR at the parking spot

Each physical spot carries a QR label. Scanning it opens the spot's live page in this website — no QR codes live on the site itself.

Maintenance console

Admins change the fault status of IR sensors, QR tags, LED strips and ESP32 units, and keep a full maintenance log.

Analytics

Occupancy, reservations per day, busiest spots and maintenance frequency — all charted live from stored data.

The prototype hardware

What powers the model

ESP32

Wireless controller per spot — heartbeat & sensor data.

TCRT5000

Reflective IR sensor detects a parked vehicle.

QR Tag

Stuck on each spot; scanned to open that spot's page here.

LED Strip

Physical status lights mirror the on-screen colour coding.
